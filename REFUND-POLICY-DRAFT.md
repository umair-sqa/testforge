# TestForge refund policy — draft for storefront review

Refund requests will be handled in accordance with the mandatory rules, buyer protections and dispute procedures of the storefront and applicable law. The product is a downloadable source-code package; customers should review the product description, prerequisites, included files and limitations before purchasing.

If a buyer cannot access the download, receives an incomplete archive, or identifies a reproducible defect in the delivered package, contact the seller through the purchase platform with the order reference and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, tokens or private application data.

This draft does not attempt to waive statutory consumer rights or override Gumroad's or Lemon Squeezy's refund and dispute rules. Confirm the applicable platform settings and local legal requirements before publishing a final policy.
